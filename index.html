THE TRAINER’S FIELD GUIDE

Know your ground.

01

Move, then make your move.

Draft six Pokémon from the shared pool first. A/D moves, W jumps. Each turn gives 180 movement points and one attack. A jump costs 18. Pick a partner with 1–6, then Q/E to choose its move.

02

Aim with confidence.

Classic aiming uses angle and power; a gold arrow always shows direction. Assisted aiming finds an arc to a clicked landing area. Hold Shift while clicking for direct aim. Flip direction with F. Dots show the predicted arc; a ring marks the impact when Full guide is enabled. The short guide shows only the launch. Power changes arcs and relay landings. Beams and Octazooka have fixed power; Dugtrio’s Dig uses facing. Space commits the attack. Zoom with +/−, right-drag to pan, or click the minimap.

03

Change the battlefield.

Most moves use long-range arcs. Relay moves send a Poké Ball to its landing marker, then summon there. Hydro Pump pushes, Earthquake breaks mountains, Dugtrio tunnels you out of a pit, Mud Bomb builds cover, salvos spread and clusters rain down. Octazooka needs a direct hit for 100 damage; a near miss does zero. Three charged beams cross the whole arena.

04

Find a combination.

Water wets for two enemy turns and extinguishes burns. Electricity deals 50% more to wet trainers. Fire burns for 4 HP on each of two turns. Curse halves the rival’s next movement budget. Uses and recharge vary by move: inspect them before drafting. Water Spout fills pits with up to eight 10-damage ticks, then drains; flat ground spills it. Ice slows targets; sludge poisons; Snarl weakens the next attack. Wet ground conducts Thunderbolt and can be frozen into temporary shelves. Ground labels show remaining turns. If all shots are exhausted, remaining HP decides the winner.

Trainers have 250–500 HP. Falls can hurt; the sea is fatal. Blast damage fades with distance and is reduced by solid cover. Pokémon are summoned attackers and do not take damage in Trainer Battle.
