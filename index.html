WELCOME TO THE HIGHLANDS

Your team. Your terrain.

Take turns on this shared keyboard. Move with A/D and jump with W. Choose a partner and move, adjust angle and power, then summon. Angles are measured from the right: 45° fires right, 135° fires left.

Each turn has a 90-pixel movement budget and one attack. Trainers have 150 HP. Falling into the sea costs the match. Craters, tunnels and new rock cover persist. Wet trainers take extra electric damage; burning lasts two turns. Aim dots predict ballistic attacks, not beams.

Every partner has a distinct role. Try pushing a rival into the sea or destroying the ground beneath them. No timer: take your time.
